Local directional correctability

χ₀ = 1 − E₁ / E₀

χ₀ > 0 means the local linearization at the starting field reduces initial error (0.5 halves it under an ideal Newton step).

Conditional convergence margin

κ₀ = 1 − βS₀E₁

κ₀ > 0 means the starting field satisfies the paper’s directional convergence criterion under local nonlinearity.6

Questions? Answers.

What surrogate model is used, and does the framework depend on it?

The paper uses a Flow-field Schrödinger Bridge (FSB) predictor, but Surrogate–Newton is architecture-agnostic: any full-field surrogate on the solver mesh can warm-start Newton–Krylov correction.

How is geometric OOD distance (d5*) computed?

For any query geometry, we compute d5 — the mean distance to its five nearest training geometries — and normalize it by the training set’s leave-one-out 99th percentile (P99,train): d5* = d5 / P99,train. Thus d5* > 1 lies beyond the training P99 boundary.

Each benchmark uses its native geometry metric: 2D airfoils use RMS wall-coordinate difference on chord-normalized profiles (27,117 training records); 3D wing sections use RMS vertical-coordinate difference across 7 control sections; 3D wing planforms use standardized Euclidean distance over sweep, aspect ratio, taper, kink fraction, and root adjustment (6,234 training wings).

How are convergence times and optimization speedups measured?

All solver comparisons use the same mesh, equations (RANS–SA in ADflow), and residual target (r ≤ 10⁻⁸ in 2D; r ≤ 10⁻⁶ in 3D). Optimization speedups compare mean active optimization time across identical seeds (17.3× transonic Top-4; 5.5× Mars UAV Top-4).

What happens on hard OOD cases that hit the time budget?

The solver never silently returns an unverified answer. It monitors the discrete equation residual directly, uses stabilized startup with optional re-prediction, and reports actual residuals if the wall-time budget is reached.

Why is a small initial residual not enough for fast convergence?

Matched residuals can hide 42× larger Field MSE (median E02, 7.65 × 10⁻³ vs 1.80 × 10⁻⁴). Pseudo-time damping lowers local residuals while leaving global flow structure far off, whereas surrogate starts satisfy the directional convergence criterion (κ₀ > 0) in 62% of OOD cases vs 9% for pseudo-time and 0% for uniform flow.

How does Top-K screening accelerate aerodynamic optimization?

Each generation, the surrogate ranks all candidates in milliseconds and NK correction runs only on the top K (e.g., 4 or 12), cutting optimization time up to 17.3× while keeping verified design quality.

Can Surrogate–Newton extend beyond fixed mesh topologies?

Current benchmarks use a shared 2D O-grid and a 13-block 3D wing mesh (~3.5M cells). Extending to varying or unstructured meshes requires compatible state transfer and effective preconditioning.

Notes & definitions (7)
  1. 2D convergence timing compares median residual-ratio trajectories across 200 paired OOD queries on 8 MPI ranks to target r ≤ 10⁻⁸: 1.76 s for Surrogate + NK vs 10.10 s for cold-start CFD (5.8× median speedup at termination). Per-case hitting-time medians including ~0.04 s neural prediction are 1.79 s vs 10.11 s (still 5.7×).
  2. 3D wing timing covers 60 paired cases on ~3.5M-cell 13-block meshes to target r ≤ 10⁻⁶: 89 s vs 472 s median elapsed time (5.2× median paired speedup). For cold-start multigrid CFD, coarse-grid relative reductions are mapped continuously in log space onto the finest-grid scale; vertical dashed lines mark grid transfers.
  3. Geometric OOD distance d5* is the mean root-mean-square wall-coordinate distance to the five nearest training geometries, normalized by the training-set leave-one-out 99th percentile (P99). Values above 1 lie outside training P99. 2D airfoil views use 1:2 vertical scaling (6.2× for thin Mars UAV sections).
  4. Field MSE is evaluated against converged CFD references using five flow and turbulence channels (ρ, u, v, p, ν̃) for 2D airfoils and nondimensional pressure p/p∞ for 3D wings. Wing errors use the original archived CFD references, with the surrogate prediction retained in four cases. Weighted Force error is EF = |ΔCL| + 10|ΔCD|.
  5. 1-NN + NK initializes Newton–Krylov from the nearest-neighbor training CFD solution (9.22 s median in 2D). In 3D pilot tests, sparse wing sampling caused 1-NN initialization to converge less reliably and more slowly than uniform initialization, so cold-start CFD serves as the primary 3D baseline.
  6. In the directional convergence criterion κ₀ = 1 − βS0E1, S0 estimates the local nonlinear response from finite probes along the initial error e0 and first Newton defect g0, and β ≥ 1 bounds directional growth along subsequent iterates. Field MSE in the matched-residual comparison is the scaled full-state MSE (E0²).
  7. Transonic (seeds 0–3) and Mars UAV (seeds 0–5) efficiency plots both report active optimization time excluding post-hoc independent CFD verification: transonic Top-4 achieves 17.3× (0.82 h vs 14.12 h; 16.3× if verification is included) and Mars UAV Top-4 achieves 5.5× (1.71 h vs 9.44 h). All reported drag and L/D metrics are independently verified by cold-start CFD. The RAE2822 optimization history uses independent CFL3D evaluations; all other benchmarks use ADflow.
