Timing and convergence on this shared research server

Timing on this shared research server. Limited hardware and queueing can make this public Demo slower than the formal benchmark setting. Cold-start ADflow convergence is case-dependent and its wall time may fluctuate substantially, typically from 10–100 s; Surrogate + NK typically takes 3–10 s. Optimization speedup is likewise strongly dependent on the case and optimization trajectory. Queue waiting time is reported separately from solver wall time. Reaching a residual target does not itself guarantee aerodynamic-coefficient accuracy.
